Self-Paced Lessons

Pick a lesson day, study each screen, answer the activities, and earn your trophy. Works on phones, tablets, and computers.

  1. Open a day and type your name and section.
  2. Tap Show more to see each part. Turn on Read to me if you like.
  3. Answer every item. You get one try, so think first.
  4. Screenshot your trophy and send it to your teacher.
Your progress is saved on this device and browser only. If you switch phones or clear your browser, start the day again.
